Maggi Recipe

Maggi is a quick and tasty snack. It is easy to make at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet of maggi
  • 1 cup of water
  • add maggi masala
  • add vegetable

instructions

  1. boil water in a pan.
  2. add maggi.
  3. add maggi masala.
  4. cook for 3-4 min.
  5. serve and enjoy it.

your delicious maggi is ready!
